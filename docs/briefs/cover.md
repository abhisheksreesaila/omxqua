# The cover page (docs/index.html), and what a theme is

## Why
- The cover has to say what omxqua believes: take the best of Apple and Google and add our own taste. Today it reads as a product page of parts, and its teal doesn't land.
- It should inspire like an Apple product launch, as omxcode's page does (http://127.0.0.1:8765/index.html).

- The tone (owner, on approval): proud of the little micro-interactions that make it a joy, each one backed by research.

## Who
- First: the owner, choosing the look for each new app. Later: people deciding whether to adopt omxqua.

## What v1 does
1. **A cover in the omxcode style:** dark only, pure black, Geist 800 headings with grey continuations, one big live product frame, mono captions, no teal. The hero leads with the belief: the best of Apple and Google, plus our taste.
2. **Interactions are the hero:** press, the stretching switch, the liquid thumb, the Beacon, each live and big enough to feel, each with one line on what we borrowed and what we added.
3. **The belief, stated:** one section on what we take from Apple, what we take from Google, and what is ours; then themes as "the cream on top" with the same base underneath.
4. **Leads into the showcase:** Playground Night, web and phone, with a theme switch.

## What a theme is
- A theme suits a kind of app, and may change colour, type face, corners, tap size, density and texture. It never changes how parts behave, move or are built, or the contrast rules.
- Each theme has a personality: its own type, corners, size and texture, not just colour. Graphite and Ledger get theirs in a follow-up brief (assumed); Playground and Azulejo already have one.
- Named by what it's for: Tech (Graphite), Kids (Playground), Travel (Azulejo), Money (Ledger). Later: Vedic, for the Vedas app. (assumed)

## Not in v1
- New components, sound on press, a new Vedic theme (its own brief when that app starts). (assumed)

## Done means
- The owner looks at the cover and calls it designy and inspiring. Every live demo works by touch at 390 px and with a mouse.
- The reviewer passes it, contrast tests pass, and it's live on GitHub Pages.

## Decisions and trade-offs
- Showcase defaults to Playground Night (owner, 2026-10-24); the other themes stay one tap away.
- Fonts stay Geist and Geist Mono, the same as omxcode. Fraunces belongs to Azulejo and Nunito to Sticker and Crayon only.
- Cover is dark only (owner): dramatic, matches omxcode; Light readers get dark.
- Hero states the belief first (owner); the live interactions follow right under it.
- Themes get a personality each (owner); giving Graphite and Ledger theirs is separate work after the cover.
