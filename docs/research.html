The research

What the research says. In one line each.

researchA study or a standard backs it. We followed it.
tasteOur choice. No study says it's better; we just think it feels right.

Each card links to the source. That's the homework, if you want it.

Touch
research
44px

Small targets are slow to hit and easy to miss.

So buttons and tabs are at least 44 px on a phone, 48 for kids.

Fitts 1954Parhi 2006WCAG 2.5.5
research
49%

Half of people tapping a phone use one hand.

The thumb reaches the bottom easily, the top corners badly. So navigation lives at the bottom.

Hoober 2013
research
0.1s

Under a tenth of a second feels instant.

So everything squashes the moment your finger lands, before any work happens.

Nielsen; Miller 1968
research
Labels

Icons on their own get misread.

So tabs keep their words, and shrink to icons only while you scroll.

NN/g, Icon usability
research
3–5tabs

Both Apple and Google stop at about five.

So the tab bar holds three to five; the rest go behind “+”.

AppleMaterial 3
research
Floats

Placeholder-only labels vanish as you type.

NN/g says keep a visible label. Ours stays; floating it into the border is our taste.

NN/g, Placeholders
Reading
research
4.5: 1

The minimum contrast for readable text.

Every theme is tested against it, in light and night, by an automated test.

WCAG 1.4.3
research
1 in 12

Men who have trouble telling some colours apart.

So a status always carries its word, never colour alone.

NEIWCAG 1.4.1
tasteresearch warns
Glass

See-through controls over busy content are harder to read.

We like it, so we allow it only on the navigation, and it turns solid when you ask for less transparency.

NN/g, Liquid GlassMDN
Motion
research
Less

Big or constant motion makes some people dizzy.

So the more often something happens, the less it moves; “reduce motion” turns it all into a short fade.

WCAG 2.3.3A11Y Project
taste
Springs

They feel good. No study shows they're faster.

So they're kept to small things under your finger: press, thumbs, toggles.

Apple, Designing Fluid Interfaces
